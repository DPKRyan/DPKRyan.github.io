Who the connection is for

JARVIS Personal AI is an owner-operated assistant for the owner and a small group of personally known users authorised by the owner. These public pages provide information about the calendar connection; they do not provide public registration or access to the assistant.

Permission and access

Only connect a Google account you control or are authorised to use. Google Calendar access is requested as read-only and is used as explained in the privacy notice. Keep account credentials and access to the local application private.

Calendar information and reminders

Calendar views and summaries support personal planning. Source calendars can change and a connection may become unavailable. Review the source calendar when confirming an important date or commitment. Local reminder delivery depends on the owner’s configuration, device availability and the relevant messaging service.

The owner controls whether optional reminders are activated and records completion of gift and birthday-message tasks. These task records do not change calendar entries or prove an action happened outside the application.

Ending access

You can revoke the Google grant through your Google Account connections and contact the owner about retained local information. The owner may disable the connection or change the available private features.

Third-party services

Google, Apple, OpenAI and GitHub operate their own services under their own terms and privacy information. This connection’s use of those services is described in the privacy notice. These information pages offer no paid subscription.
