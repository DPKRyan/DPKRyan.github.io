Personal calendar connection

A clearer view of the day ahead.

JARVIS Personal AI is an owner-operated personal assistant. Its Google Calendar connection helps its owner review upcoming events and prepare useful schedule summaries.

Private tool, public information. These pages explain the connection. Access to the assistant is arranged by its owner; there is no public registration or calendar login on this website.

Calendar information, brought together

The connection uses Google Calendar read-only access. JARVIS is configured to read calendars selected by its owner, including a personal calendar and a shared family calendar.

Selected Apple calendars can also contribute to the owner’s local calendar view. The calendar board uses event identities to recognise confirmed linked copies and leaves uncertain matches for review.

Reminders under owner control

The combined calendar board and local reminder workflow are prepared for owner review. The optional reminder workflow supports birthday planning and separate gift-bought and birthday-message progress. Reminder delivery needs the owner’s explicit activation.

Local iMessage delivery relies on the owner’s Mac being available and signed into Messages.

Understand the data use

Calendar details are processed in the owner’s local application. The existing voice-assistant feature can supply a limited calendar summary to its AI provider when a voice session is started. The separate calendar board and birthday scheduler process their calendar information locally.

Read the calendar privacy notice for the information accessed, storage, optional sharing and how to disconnect. The use terms explain access and owner responsibility.
