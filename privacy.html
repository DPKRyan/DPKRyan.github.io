Calendar privacy notice

How calendar information is used.

Updated 29 October 2026

JARVIS Personal AI is an owner-operated personal assistant for a small group of personally known, authorised users. This notice covers its Google Calendar connection and related calendar summaries and reminders. The owner can be contacted at rcam21sv@gmail.com.

Information accessed

The connection requests Google Calendar read-only permission. That permission allows access to calendar metadata and event information, which can include calendar names and identifiers, event titles and descriptions, dates and times, locations, event identifiers, availability or cancellation status, and invitation or attendee details where present.

JARVIS is configured to use calendars selected by its owner. When enabled, its bounded calendar board requests the schedule fields it needs, primarily titles, dates and times, locations, status and identifiers. Existing calendar-reading features may receive a fuller event response from Google.

Why the information is used

Calendar information supports the owner’s daily and upcoming-event views, schedule summaries, recognition of linked calendar copies and optional birthday or gift reminders. Calendar read-only permission does not authorise this connection to create, edit or delete Google events.

Selected Apple calendar information can be combined locally with selected Google events. Reminder preferences, gift-bought and birthday-message completion, and notification-attempt history are kept separately from the source calendars.

Storage and retention

Google access credentials and calendar snapshots are stored in the owner’s local application environment. Refreshed snapshots replace older derived views as the application checks its sources. Local review evidence, assistant context, operational history and any owner-managed backups may remain until the owner removes them.

Completing a birthday task removes it from the active view while retaining its history. Notification-attempt records are retained to prevent an uncertain send being repeated. Disconnecting Google stops future authorised reads; it does not itself erase information already saved locally or in an owner-managed backup.

Sharing and the optional voice feature

When an owner starts the existing OpenAI-powered voice-assistant session, a limited calendar summary for the upcoming day can be supplied to OpenAI as context for that session. The summary contains schedule details such as event titles, times and source labels. The separate calendar board and birthday scheduler process their calendar information locally rather than sending it to an AI model.

When owner-enabled reminder delivery is active, the relevant reminder text is sent through Apple Messages to the owner-configured recipient. The public information website does not receive calendar records, message histories or Google access credentials.

Selling calendar information, advertising with it and training a general-purpose AI model are not functions of this calendar connection. JARVIS Personal AI’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

An authorised user can remove the JARVIS connection from their Google Account connections. The owner can also disable the calendar connection or local reminders in the application’s configuration. To request removal of locally retained calendar information, contact the owner; any retained send-attempt history and backup copies must also be considered.

This information website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script or calendar-data collection. GitHub Pages may process visitor connection information, including IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and changes

Contact rcam21sv@gmail.com about this connection or notice. Material changes to calendar-data use will be reflected here and brought to authorised users’ attention before that new use begins.
